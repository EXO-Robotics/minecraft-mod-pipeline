# Observe one Bedrock factory pack

Remain independent of production and semantic disposition. Collect evidence;
do not edit the candidate, authorize repair, or declare T10 equivalence.

## Execute

1. Verify the admitted candidate generation, commit/tree, package SHA-256,
   manifests, runtime binary/image, fixture, scenario, collector, and dependency
   lock. Stop on mismatch.
2. Preserve a candidate-only baseline proving load, initial state, save,
   shutdown, and restart without test packs.
3. Run instrumentation only in a separate disposable fixture with the exact
   same candidate. Keep observer and GameTest packs outside the `.mcaddon`.
4. Calibrate instrumentation against baseline. Quarantine evidence if it alters
   candidate bytes, candidate-owned state, resources, progression, errors,
   spawning, namespace, or timing outside declared bounds.
5. Use the minimum acceptable actor for each assertion. Label retail clients,
   protocol clients, SimulatedPlayers, fake players, administrative commands,
   and fixtures separately; never promote one actor beyond its calibration.
6. For network scenarios, exercise bounded join/spawn, inventory/state
   snapshot, disconnect/reconnect, restart/reconnect, two identities, duplicate
   delivery, and contract-authorized malformed/old state as applicable.
7. Emit normalized immutable observations with capabilities, limitations, and
   explicit `OBSERVED_TRUE`, `OBSERVED_FALSE`, `NOT_OBSERVED`,
   `UNSUPPORTED_BY_ADAPTER`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `CLIENT_REQUIRED` fields.
8. Publish evidence metadata and a sanitized mailbox-addressable result for
   T10. Keep private Java evidence, private adapters, and hidden cases outside
   production messages.
9. Remove only task-labeled containers, networks, volumes, ports, fixture
   clones, and leases; verify zero task-owned runtime objects remain.

Keep Stable and Preview lifecycle results distinct from their network-player
semantic results. Offline protocol identities may prove local event delivery,
reconnect, inventory observation, and player/world scope; they do not prove
authenticated XUID, Xbox persistence, Realms, retail UI, controller,
split-screen, rendering, audio, or physical PS4.

Return `OBSERVATIONS_READY_FOR_T10`, `ORACLE_INSUFFICIENT`, `CLIENT_REQUIRED`,
`INCONCLUSIVE`, or `INFRASTRUCTURE_BLOCKED` with exact evidence hashes. Never
turn collector inability or infrastructure failure into a product defect.
