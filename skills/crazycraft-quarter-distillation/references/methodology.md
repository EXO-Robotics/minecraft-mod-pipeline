# Methodology

Inventory version-pinned artifacts, metadata, source/JAR evidence, dependencies,
registrations, behaviors, assets, configs, scripts, rights, and unsupported
hooks. Cluster those facts into player-facing systems, not files or raw
registration counts.

For each system, answer where it is encountered, what it rewards and unlocks,
what depends on it, what future content can reuse it, and whether it can be
removed without breaking progression.

Use fixed integer scores and deterministic lexical tie-breaking. Form
prerequisite-closed bundles before comparing them. Enforce conversion effort,
static console cost, stable APIs, rights, dependencies, and complete progression.

Normally cover early survival → unusual equipment → regional creatures →
structures → elites → boss materials → advanced armor/powers → world-tier
bosses → final chaos encounter → postgame modifiers. Declare a different
evidence-backed path explicitly.

Keep deterministic results immutable. Record review adjustments separately with
author, authority, reason, evidence, prior value, and proposed value. Revalidate
all constraints after any reviewed scope change.
