# Output contract

Require these files under `distillation/`:

- `quarter-scope.yaml`
- `quarter-scope.json`
- `deferred-scope.yaml`
- `identity-map.md`
- `system-clusters.json`
- `scoring-report.json`
- `progression-graph.json`
- `conversion-roadmap.md`
- `pattern-coverage.json`
- `compiler-gap-analysis.md`
- `console-performance-risk.json`
- `rights-risk.json`
- `benchmark-plan.md`
- `executive-summary.md`

`distillation-manifest.json` may additionally record source, policy, and artifact
digests. JSON-compatible YAML is intentional in this dependency-light
repository.

Accept output only when dependencies are closed, budgets hold, progression is
complete, every decision is explained, category omissions are flagged, review
is separate, and no rights, Marketplace, or measured-console claim is inferred.
