# Rights and originality

Technical reconstruction does not establish distribution rights. Evaluate code,
assets, branding, trademarks, derivative permission, commercial use, and
Marketplace review separately.

Use `DIRECT_RECONSTRUCTION`, `BEDROCK_NATIVE_REDESIGN`,
`ORIGINAL_REPLACEMENT`, `DEFER`, `UNSUPPORTED`, or `RIGHTS_BLOCKED`.

Unknown rights block direct reconstruction. Original replacement may preserve
only high-level fantasy and mechanical role. Do not copy protected names,
characters, models, textures, sounds, structures, branding, lore, or distinctive
creative expression.

Neither AI review nor clean-room implementation can grant Marketplace clearance.
Require attributable human review and the actual Marketplace process.
