# Rights and provenance

Rights review is a hard pre-package gate for a Marketplace candidate and remains separate from technical conversion.

The rights manifest records source code, textures, models, animations, sounds, music, structures, characters, names, logos, branding, trademarks, documentation, and dependencies separately. Each record identifies the work, owner, license or permission, derivative/commercial/Marketplace scope, attribution, evidence paths, and decision.

Allowed states are `UNKNOWN`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `PERSONAL_USE_ONLY`, `ATTRIBUTION_REQUIRED`, `PERMISSION_REQUIRED`, `MARKETPLACE_CLEARED`, and `EXCLUDE`. Only an accountable human can assign `MARKETPLACE_CLEARED`. An open-source code license does not establish rights to assets, branding, trademarks, or Marketplace distribution.

Uncleared material is excluded or blocks candidate packaging. Automated scanning may find license files and conflicts but must not issue legal approval. Review records identify reviewer, date, evidence, scope, and content hashes. Re-review is required if inputs or intended distribution change.

Benchmark A uses an original-authorship declaration but still requires a human gate before any commercial claim. Benchmark B remains unselected until all categories are evidenced; see its selection scaffold.
