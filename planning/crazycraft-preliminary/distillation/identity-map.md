No exact Crazy Craft distribution inventory is available, so these are original category-level replacement proposals, not findings about named source content.

## Inputs required for full analysis

- A human-reviewed player-facing inventory and progression map.
- A legally obtained, exact version-pinned Crazy Craft distribution or complete artifact manifest.
- Human-attributable Marketplace distribution review records.
- Loader and Minecraft versions plus dependency, side-only, mixin, coremod, and networking metadata.
- Per-component code licenses and separate asset, sound, model, texture, branding, trademark, derivative, and commercial permissions.
- SHA-256 hashes for every JAR, nested JAR, config, script, and resource archive.
- Source repositories or decompilation permission for semantic analysis where lawful.
- Target-console profiling evidence after original prototypes exist.
