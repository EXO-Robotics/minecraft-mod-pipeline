# Aionbound G8 structure playtest

Install `aionbound-wave-1-g8-structure-playtest.mcaddon`, create a new Creative
world, activate both included packs, and enable cheats. Use a broad, prepared
area; neither structure is registered for natural generation in this package.

## Hunter Lodge

Stand at the desired lower structure origin and run:

```text
/structure load aionbound:hunter_lodge_g2 ~ ~ ~ 0_degrees none
```

The lodge is `24 x 16 x 20`. Its exact structure bytes passed complete-volume
Stable BDS settlement and restart scans and the declared terrain matrix.

## Grand Stone Castle

Stand at the intended approach position on a large, flat test pad and run:

```text
/structure load aionbound:grand_stone_castle ~-32 ~-5 ~-3 0_degrees none
```

The castle is `64 x 48 x 64`. The relative command aligns its authored ground
datum and approach with the player. Give the full footprint room to load and
remain nearby while it settles.

## Review checklist

- Check both structures from a distance and at ground level.
- Walk every entrance, stair, tower, room, bridge, and wall walk.
- Verify the castle moat, drawbridge, gatehouse, keep, towers, chapel, barracks,
  stables, kitchen, dungeon, treasury, postern, and roof access.
- Record the client version, platform, input device, rotation, placement
  coordinates, screenshots, and any inaccessible or visually broken area.

This is an engineering playtest handoff. It is not a release candidate and does
not claim client, controller, multiplayer, console, Realm, Marketplace, or
release qualification.
