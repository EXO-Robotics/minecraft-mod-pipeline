# W1-CREATIVE-003 — Thorn Court Behavior Proposal

Status: **PROPOSED_NOT_RATIFIED**  
Authority effect: **NONE UNTIL RATIFIED**

The proposal turns the approved four-beat fantasy into one bounded arena encounter without granting boss authority to naturally spawned Thorn Stalkers.

Proposed constants are 360 solo health, +35% base health per additional locked participant (maximum four), phase transitions at 70%, 35%, and 10%, a six-minute soft-enrage ceiling, a 48-block leash, and bounded adds. The JSON sibling fixes every telegraph, active, recovery, cooldown, reset, join, disconnect, reload, and completion rule.

Active fights do not survive a world restart: the encounter returns to a clean, unpulled state. Durable state is limited to world completion, per-player completion, and per-player reward entitlement. Repeat clears remain playable but cannot duplicate chapter progression or seal entitlement.

This proposal names no new reward. Ticket 004 remains the sole numeric loot and entitlement authority.

Approval is required for health/scaling, thresholds/timing, reset behavior, multiplayer eligibility, and persistence/terminal semantics.
