# W1-CREATIVE-006 — Whisperwood Sapling Regrowth Proposal

Status: **PROPOSED_NOT_RATIFIED**  
Authority effect: **NONE UNTIL RATIFIED**

The Creative contract says `whisperwood_sapling` provides renewable tree growth, but no standing-tree assembly or growth envelope is bound. The current runtime therefore registers and places the sapling without pretending it can regrow a tree.

This proposal authorizes one conservative, non-landmark standing Whisperwood tree assembled only from the existing log, leaves, roots, and moss-bark blocks. It creates no inventory identity, resource, loot table, entity, or cross-region dependency.

Natural growth occurs within a 12–30 loaded-minute envelope. Bone meal may accelerate one bounded attempt but does not guarantee growth. An obstructed or unsupported attempt leaves the sapling intact and retries later. Engineering may select a stable Bedrock mechanism, but it may not change the palette, silhouette envelope, timing, or drop identity.

Approval is required for the assembly envelope and growth timing before implementation.
