# Ashen Creature Audio

Status: `PASS_STATIC_EARLY_PLACEHOLDER_AUDIO`

All ten Ashen creatures have ambient, hurt, and death mappings plus bounded
ambient intervals. The pass deliberately reuses reviewed vanilla event IDs and
varies pitch, volume, cadence, and audible range by Ashen role. It introduces no
audio bytes and does not claim custom signature cues, client playback, BDS, or
console evidence. Final authored audio remains a later presentation obligation.
