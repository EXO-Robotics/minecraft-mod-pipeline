All ten Packet 002 full-cube blocks now have explicit harvest tables. The
regional natural-block layer supplies sulfur, volcanic glass shards, ember
resin, heatstone, basalt core, and ash crystal. Every break selects exactly one
outcome; no table returns a placeable block plus a bonus resource, preventing
re-break duplication. Smolder bark is refined from logs, while charbone and
furnace chitin remain creature/structure-derived and fire bloom seed remains
plant/creature-derived, preserving provenance instead of making every material
an ore.

distribution and player-observed harvest behavior remain later integrated proof.
