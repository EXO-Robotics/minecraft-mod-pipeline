# Crystal Marsh plant ecology

Ten bounded feature/rule pairs express Packet 003 wetland locality through a
swamp-or-river regional proxy. Surface plants, shallow-water plants, and two
submerged identities use different placement inputs. The Crystal tuning is not
copied from Whisperwood or Ashen, and aggregate pre-filter attempts remain at
or below `1.0` per chunk with no runtime-cap increase.

Water replacement is permitted only for plants whose custom block definition
explicitly contains water. This is static source registration; regional tag
selection, live placement, water behavior, density, rendering, BDS, and console
performance remain unproven until their established gates.
